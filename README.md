# Tatpulse Privacy Policy

Hosts the privacy policy for the **Tatpulse Forwarder** Android application,
published by Enetro Private Limited.

Live: https://enetroai.github.io/tatpulse-privacy/

The site is a single static page (`index.html`) served via GitHub Pages from
this repository's `main` branch. To update the policy, edit `index.html`,
commit, and push to `main`. Pages will redeploy within a minute or two.

## Why this lives separately from www.enetro.ai

The Tatpulse Forwarder app needs a publicly reachable privacy policy URL for
its Google Play Console listing. Hosting the policy here keeps it
independent of the marketing site's release cycle and makes it easy to
update when the app's data handling changes.

The general Enetro privacy policy still lives at
https://www.enetro.ai/privacy-policy and is referenced from this page.
