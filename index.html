Who we are

Enetro Private Limited ("Enetro", "we", "us") is the developer of the TATPulse Forwarder application for Android and iOS ("the app"). The app is a B2B tool that routes one-time passcodes (OTPs) from operational mobile phones to authorised back-office workflows, on behalf of the organisation that has installed and paired it.

For questions about this policy, contact info@enetro.ai.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the data handling of the TATPulse Forwarder application, on Android and on iOS. Where the two differ, the difference is stated. It is separate from, and supplements, Enetro's general privacy policy for the corporate website and web platform at https://www.enetro.ai/privacy-policy.

What the app does

On Android: when a TATPulse administrator pairs an Android device with the app, the device begins watching for incoming SMS messages whose sender ID and content match patterns the administrator has configured in the workflow. Matching messages, identified as one-time passcodes (OTPs), are forwarded to Enetro's servers and routed onward to the administrator-configured destination. SMS messages that do not match a configured pattern are ignored entirely and never leave the device.

On iOS: no iOS app can read SMS. After pairing, the person setting up the phone creates a Shortcuts automation by hand (a Message trigger with a text filter, set to Run Immediately) that hands the text of each incoming message it matches to the app's Forward Message action. The automation sees every message in the Messages app that its filter matches, whether it arrived as SMS, RCS or iMessage, and the filter the app's setup steps use (a single space) matches nearly every message. iOS gives the app no sender, so rules match the message text alone. The app matches the text on the phone; text that matches no rule is neither sent nor stored, and for a match only the extracted code leaves the phone, never the message. Each message the automation hands over also makes the app contact the server for each paired workspace (a status heartbeat and a rules check), even when no rule matches; neither request carries the message.

Not collected

  • Messages (SMS, RCS or iMessage) that match no configured rule; on iOS the message text and its sender are never sent, matched or not
  • Phone contacts
  • Location
  • Photos, files, or other on-device content
  • Microphone or camera input
  • Browsing history
  • On iOS, the phone's own number and the Apple Account

How we use the data

  • Forwarding OTPs to the destination configured by the administrator (typically a webhook into the customer's own systems).
  • De-duplicating OTPs so the same code is not delivered twice when multiple SIMs receive the same SMS.
  • Showing diagnostics to the device operator (recent deliveries, retry counts) and to administrators on the TATPulse web console.
  • Diagnosing app problems through anonymised crash reports (Android only).

We do not use OTP or SMS data for advertising, profiling, training machine-learning models, or any purpose unrelated to the workflow you have authorised. We do not sell personal data.

Push notifications and crash reporting (Android only)

The iOS app uses no push service and no crash reporting, so workflow changes, renames and revocations reach an iPhone the next time it contacts the server (with the next message, when the app opens, or at background refresh).

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) (Android only)

The app uses FCM to receive wake-up signals from our servers (for example when workflow rules change, when the device is asked to re-authenticate, or when an instance is revoked). FCM processes the device's registration token. Google's handling of FCM data is governed by Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Firebase Crashlytics (Android only)

The app uses Crashlytics to collect anonymised crash reports if it misbehaves. We do not include OTP values, SMS bodies, or sender identifiers in crash reports.

Third-party sharing

We share data only with:

  • The back-office system the administrator has configured as the workflow destination (this is the entire point of the app).
  • Google Firebase, on Android only, for FCM push notifications and Crashlytics crash reporting (see https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (Amazon Web Services, hosted in India) that operate our servers.

The iOS app sends data to no one but Enetro's servers.

We do not sell data to data brokers, share OTPs with advertisers, or disclose data to anyone other than the parties above except where required by law.

User rights

The TATPulse Forwarder app is intended to be installed by an organisation on its own devices, not by individual end users. The organisation that paired the device is the data controller; Enetro processes the OTP data on behalf of that organisation.

If you are an end user of a device that has TATPulse Forwarder installed, please contact your organisation's TATPulse administrator for any data-subject request (access, correction, deletion).

If you are an organisation customer, contact us at info@enetro.ai with data-subject requests routed through your administrator account.

Your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

If you are a Data Principal whose personal data is processed by the TATPulse Forwarder app, you have the following rights under the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Right to access a summary of personal data processed about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to correction, completion, updating, and erasure of your personal data.
  • Right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of incapacity or death.
  • Right to grievance redressal through the contact below.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time. In this app, withdrawal is effected by tapping Unpair on each instance, or uninstalling the app. On iOS, withdrawal is effected by Unpair (swipe a workspace on Home, or Unpair on its screen), deleting the Shortcuts automation, or deleting the app (see "Removing the app" below).

Because the device is paired by your organisation, the organisation is the Data Fiduciary for the OTPs collected on that device, and Enetro Private Limited acts as a Data Processor on its behalf. The first point of contact for any rights request is your organisation's TATPulse administrator.

Cross-border transfer

OTP data is processed and stored on servers located in India. On Android, the following limited metadata is processed by Google LLC outside India, under Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy:

  • FCM registration tokens (used to deliver push notifications to the device).
  • Anonymised crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).

On iOS, the app's data goes only to Enetro's servers in India. These transfers are made only for the purposes described in this policy and only to jurisdictions India has not restricted under the DPDP Act.

Removing the app

To stop further OTP capture immediately:

  1. Open the app and tap Unpair on each instance, or
  2. Uninstall the app from the device.
  3. On iOS, also delete the Message automation in Shortcuts, under Automation.

On iOS, deleting the app removes its pairings and journal, while iOS keeps the app's Keychain items (the install identifier, and the credential of any workspace not unpaired first), which work only with a new pairing. Unpair first to revoke the credential on the server.

Server-side OTP data tied to the unpaired device is purged within 30 days of unpair or uninstall.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • The app validates server certificates and rejects connections to servers presenting invalid certificates.
  • API keys distributed to the app are scoped to a single device and workflow, and are rotated on revocation events.
  • Crashlytics crash reports (Android only) are sent over HTTPS to Firebase.
  • On iOS, credentials are Keychain items bound to this device and never in a backup, and the app's stored data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

Children

The app is not intended for or directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
