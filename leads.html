Who we are

Enetro Private Limited ("Enetro", "we", "us") is the developer of the TATPulse Leads application for Android and iOS ("the app"). The app is a B2B field-sales tool that delivers time-critical sales-lead alerts from a dealership's TATPulse workspace to the paired device of a sales representative, reports the representative's responses (claims, calls, outcomes) back to that workspace, and, where the dealership has set up a field form, lets the representative file a lead for a customer they met in person.

For questions about this policy, contact info@enetro.ai.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the data handling of the TATPulse Leads application, on Android and on iOS. Where the two differ, the difference is stated. It is separate from, and supplements, the policy for the TATPulse Forwarder app at this site's index page and Enetro's general privacy policy for the corporate website and web platform at https://www.enetro.ai/privacy-policy.

What the app does

A dealership administrator pairs a sales representative's device with the app using a one-time pairing code generated in the dealership's TATPulse workspace; there is no consumer sign-up. Once paired, the device receives sales-lead alerts in real time (via push notifications and a persistent connection), the representative can claim a lead and call the customer directly from the alert, and the app reports the claim and the call outcome back to the dealership's lead timeline. While the representative is on shift, the app maintains a lightweight presence heartbeat so the dealership's system knows the device is reachable before dispatching a lead to it (on iOS the heartbeat runs only while the app is open or an alert is being delivered).

Where the dealership has set up a field form, the app shows a New lead button. The representative fills in the form for a customer they met in person: the customer's details, the form's other answers, and, where the form asks for them, photos, files and the phone's location. The lead is then created in the dealership's workspace, and the representative can follow it in Filed by me.

Call log access (optional)

The Call Log permission is optional and is requested on its own clearly-marked card inside the app. Declining it does not restrict the app: lead alerts, claiming, and dialing all work without it. When granted, the app reads only the log entry of the call the representative just placed to the customer of a lead they claimed: the call's time, duration, and whether it connected: to (a) show first/last-contacted history on that lead and (b) record the sales call in the dealership's lead timeline. The permission can be withdrawn at any time in Android system settings, and the app continues to work.

How we use the data

  • Delivering lead alerts to the paired device and routing each lead to the representative who claims it.
  • Updating the dealership's lead timeline with claims, calls, and outcomes so the dealership can follow up on its own sales leads.
  • Showing contact history (first/last called) on a lead to the representative handling it.
  • Presence and availability: dispatching leads only to devices that are on shift and reachable.
  • Creating leads the representative files with a field form, with the photos, files and location the form asks for.
  • Diagnosing problems through alert-delivery diagnostics and, on Android, anonymised crash reports.

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, training machine-learning models, or any purpose unrelated to the dealership workspace the device is paired to. We do not sell personal data.

Push notifications and crash reporting

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Apple Push Notification service (APNs)

The app uses FCM to deliver lead alerts and wake-up signals from our servers (for example when a new lead is dispatched, when the device is asked to re-authenticate, or when a pairing is revoked); on iOS, FCM hands the alerts to Apple's APNs, which delivers them to the device. Both process the device's push token. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. Google's handling of FCM data is governed by Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Firebase Crashlytics (Android only)

The Android app uses Crashlytics to collect anonymised crash reports if it misbehaves. We do not include customer phone numbers, call log entries, or lead contents in crash reports.

Third-party sharing

We share data only with:

  • The dealership workspace the device is paired to, including the dealership's own CRM or lead-management destinations its administrator has configured (this is the entire point of the app).
  • Google Firebase for FCM push notifications and Crashlytics crash reporting (see https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Apple, on iOS, for push notification delivery through APNs (see https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (Amazon Web Services, hosted in India) that operate our servers and store the photos and files filed with a form.

We do not sell data to data brokers, share call logs or lead data with advertisers, or disclose data to anyone other than the parties above except where required by law.

User rights

The TATPulse Leads app is intended to be installed on devices used by a dealership's own sales staff, provisioned by the dealership's administrator; it is not a consumer app. The dealership that paired the device is the data controller; Enetro processes the data on behalf of that dealership.

If you are a sales representative using a device with TATPulse Leads installed, please contact your dealership's TATPulse administrator for any data-subject request (access, correction, deletion).

If you are a dealership customer, contact us at info@enetro.ai with data-subject requests routed through your administrator account.

Your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

If you are a Data Principal whose personal data is processed by the TATPulse Leads app, you have the following rights under the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Right to access a summary of personal data processed about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to correction, completion, updating, and erasure of your personal data.
  • Right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of incapacity or death.
  • Right to grievance redressal through the contact below.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time. In this app, withdrawal is effected by revoking the optional Call Log permission (Android) or the camera or location permission in the system settings, unpairing the device, or uninstalling the app (see "Removing the app" below).

Because the device is paired by your dealership, the dealership is the Data Fiduciary for the lead, call and field-form data collected on that device, and Enetro Private Limited acts as a Data Processor on its behalf. The first point of contact for any rights request is your dealership's TATPulse administrator. A customer whose details a representative filed with a field form can make the same request through the dealership.

Cross-border transfer

Lead, call-report and field-form data, its photos and files included, is processed and stored on servers located in India. The following limited metadata is processed by Google LLC outside India, under Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy:

  • FCM registration tokens (used to deliver lead alerts to the device).
  • Anonymised crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Android only).
  • On iOS, push tokens processed by Apple Inc. to deliver alerts through APNs.

These transfers are made only for the purposes described in this policy and only to jurisdictions India has not restricted under the DPDP Act.

Removing the app

To stop further data collection immediately:

  1. Open the app and unpair the device from its workspace, or
  2. Uninstall the app from the device.

Server-side device data (pairing, presence, FCM token, diagnostics) tied to the unpaired device is purged within 30 days of unpair or uninstall. Lead records and call reports already written into the dealership's lead timeline remain part of the dealership's own business records, under the dealership's control.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • The app validates server certificates and rejects connections to servers presenting invalid certificates.
  • API keys distributed to the app are scoped to a single device and workspace, and are rotated on revocation events.
  • Credentials on the device are stored in encrypted storage (the Android Keystore, the iOS Keychain).
  • Photos and files filed with a form are stored encrypted at rest.
  • Crashlytics crash reports (Android) are sent over HTTPS to Firebase.

Children

The app is not intended for or directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
